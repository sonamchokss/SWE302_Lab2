What you can do

Four tools every CST student needs, built from the Lab 1 requirements.

Secure login

Sign in with your 8-digit Student ID. Your session is protected and your records stay private.

Tuition payment

Upload your payment screenshot and transaction number to get an electronic receipt.

Module registration

Pick your modules once payment and drug-test checks are cleared and the period is open.

Result viewing

See your grades for every registered module as soon as they are published.

How it works

From login to results in three steps.

Log in

Use your Student ID and password to open your personal dashboard.

Pay & register

Submit your tuition proof, then choose the modules you are taking this semester.

Track results

Come back any time to see your modules and grades.
